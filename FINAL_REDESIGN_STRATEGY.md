# Kabira Final Website Strategy

## Direction

The final design combines the previous site’s visual continuity and richer storytelling with the newer version’s stronger hierarchy, conversion path, mobile navigation, and editorial polish.

The visual system uses consistent rectangular image frames with restrained rounded corners. Decorative thread lines remain outside text and image content. The earlier arch-shaped masks have been removed because they created uneven cropping and could collide with nearby content.

Typography is larger and fuller across desktop and mobile. Sections alternate between navy, green, warm cream, and white in a repeatable rhythm so the three pages feel like one connected experience.

## Three-page sitemap

1. **Home** — emotional promise, trust signals, parent reasons, program overview, Dr. Rita Rattan, learning and campus preview, the Kabira story, and visit CTA.
2. **The Kabira Experience** — philosophy, Pre-Nursery to UKG details, the learning day, daycare, planned campus and safety, values, long-term growth, and the full Director & Principal message.
3. **Admissions & Visit** — age guide, admission steps, visit conversation, documents, FAQ, visit planner, address, and map.

## Naming and leadership

Every public reference uses **Dr. Rita Rattan** and **Director & Principal**. Her nearly 28 years in teaching, administration, and school leadership form the central human trust signal.

## Conversion path

The mobile header keeps Admissions visible. A compact mobile action dock links directly to Programs and Plan a Visit. The primary flow is:

**Understand the promise → see evidence and leadership → find the right program → plan a school visit.**

No school phone number, email address, `tel:` link, `mailto:` link, or WhatsApp destination is included.

## Visual consistency

- One image treatment with natural aspect ratios and no forced cropping.
- One card radius, one border style, and one spacing scale.
- Alternating full-width colour bands create a stronger visual rhythm.
- Illustrative school scenes are clearly labelled until real campus photography is available.
- Fine weave lines and restrained gold accents reference Sant Kabir without becoming literal or decorative clutter.

## Performance and accessibility

The site remains static and framework-free with one stylesheet and one small JavaScript file. It uses responsive local images, deferred loading below the fold, explicit image dimensions, system fonts, semantic landmarks, visible focus states, labelled form controls, reduced-motion support, and one H1 per page.
